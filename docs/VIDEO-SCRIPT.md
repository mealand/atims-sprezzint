# ATiMs Founder Video Script — 60 seconds

**Format:** founder on camera (talking head, optional b-roll of farms/market/phone
screens over the middle section). One take, natural speech. Bracketed [cuts] show
where screen recordings or b-roll can cover.

Target: ~150 words total (1 minute at natural pace). Times are cumulative targets.

---

**[0:00–0:10] Who I am**

> "I'm [name], founder of Sprezz Integrated Limited. We build ATiMs — a
> traceability system for agricultural exports, live today at
> atims.sprezzint.com."

**[0:10–0:25] The problem** [b-roll: crate of produce, export paperwork]

> "Every year, exporters lose entire consignments — not because the produce is
> bad, but because the paperwork can't prove where it came from and how it was
> handled. Existing traceability tools just *record* what happened. They don't
> stop an under-documented batch from moving."

**[0:25–0:40] The product** [screen: stage gate blocking; QR trace page verifying]

> "ATiMs does. A batch physically cannot advance to its next stage — harvest,
> aggregation, packing, export — until the required evidence, a certificate or
> lab result, is on file. And every custody event is cryptographically signed
> and chained, so anyone — a buyer, a regulator, a consumer — can scan a QR code
> and verify the full history themselves, without trusting us."

**[0:40–0:52] Why it's credible** [screen: open-source repo / "100% open-source" badge]

> "It's built entirely on open-source software with zero paid services, so any
> cooperative, ministry, or partner can run it themselves and keep their data
> sovereign. That openness is the trust."

**[0:52–1:00] The ask**

> "We're looking to pilot one export corridor end-to-end with the Alliance.
> Give us a corridor and a cooperative — and we'll show you rejection rates
> fall. Thank you."

---

## Delivery notes

- Total ≈ 150 words. Rehearse once with a timer; if over, trim the b-roll
  narration first, never the ask.
- Say the domain **once** (`atims.sprezzint.com`) and the QR moment is the visual
  proof — have the live trace page ready on your phone.
- Smile at "Thank you" and hold eye contact for the last second — Alliance
  reviewers often freeze the final frame.
- If recording with the product on screen: stage 1 → dark backdrop, phone in
  hand showing a green "verified" trace; keep the Sprezz crimson (#A81D1D)
  visible somewhere in the frame (shirt/background/logo) for brand recall.
